**Status:** Solo-authored working paper (U.S. Ph.D. writing sample)

**Abstract:** This paper studies how exchange-rate volatility affects the return on invested capital (ROIC) of Korean manufacturing exporters, using an administrative firm-level panel covering more than 971,000 firm-years from 2016–2022 (KOSI). I document a U-shaped relationship between FX volatility and ROIC and a five-year structural depression in performance following entry, attributable to sunk entry costs. The empirical strategy combines Correlated Random Effects, Staggered Difference-in-Differences, System GMM, and Quantile Regression to establish these results across the firm performance distribution.
