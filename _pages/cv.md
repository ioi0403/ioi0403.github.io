Working Papers
======
* **"When Volatility Pays: U-Shaped Exchange-Rate Exposure and the Option Value of Exporting in Korean Manufacturing"** (2026). Solo-authored working paper; U.S. Ph.D. writing sample.
  * Findings: Identifies a U-shaped FX volatility effect on ROIC and a 5-year structural depression from entry sunk costs.
  * Data: Administrative microdata panel (971,000+ firm-years, 2016–2022) via KOSI.
  * Methods: Correlated Random Effects, Staggered DiD, System GMM, Quantile Regression.

* **"Power of Forward Guidance under Quasi-Hyperbolic Preferences"** (2026). Joint with Daeha Cho (Hanyang University). *The Korean Economic Review* (SSCI), under review.
  * Findings: Resolves the forward guidance puzzle via quasi-hyperbolic discounting in a New Keynesian model.
  * Implications: Finds a neutral effect on optimal ZLB duration due to offsetting recession-severity and policy-effectiveness.
  * Methods: Non-linear DSGE (MATLAB/Dynare), analytical determinacy, optimal targeting rules.
