Working Papers
======
* **"When Volatility Pays: Nonlinear Effect of Exchange Rate Volatility on Firm Profitability"** (2026). Solo-authored. [**Writing Sample**](/files/Writing_Sample_When_Volatility_Pays_Hyeongjin_Choi.pdf)
  * Combines the risk-aversion and real-option channels in one model: volatility raises both the option value of exporting and its risk cost, but at different speeds, so the marginal return to exporting is U-shaped in volatility among SMEs; a linear specification finds no effect.
  * Uses nearly one million firm-year observations of Korean manufacturing firms (2016–2022); because volatility varies only across years, inference relies on year-specific slopes and a permutation test.

* **"Power of Forward Guidance under Quasi-Hyperbolic Preferences"** (2026). Joint with Daeha Cho (Hanyang University). Under review, *The Korean Economic Review* (submitted Sep 2026). Revised and extended version of my M.A. thesis (2024).
  * Quasi-hyperbolic discounting halves the impact inflation response to distant forward guidance; combined with a discounted Euler equation, it also mitigates the output-gap response, leaving the optimal zero-rate duration nearly unchanged.
  * Present bias widens the set of policy rules delivering a unique equilibrium and worsens the inflation–output trade-off after a cost-push shock.
